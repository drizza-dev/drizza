Open-source guide

Accounts and local data

The open-source local app does not require a Drizza account.

Using Drizza locally

You do not need a Drizza account to run the open-source local app. Drizza runs on your machine, and its local SQLite database is stored at ~/.drizza/drizza.db.

Provider accounts

GitHub, MCP, and Slack integrations are not available in this guide yet. Their connection steps, permissions, and token-revocation instructions will be documented when the corresponding integrations are ready.

Do not paste credentials into this guide or into an issue. Wait for the provider-specific instructions before connecting an account.

Local privacy

The local server binds to 127.0.0.1. The planned behavior for provider integrations is for the local process to use credentials directly with providers, without writing them to the Drizza SQLite database or logs. Tokens are planned to stay out of Drizza-hosted services.
